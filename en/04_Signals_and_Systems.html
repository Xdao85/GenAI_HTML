Level E · SIG-E

Elementary Signals and Time Operations

These operations act on the time axis, not on the signal values: $x(t-2)$ is a delay (it moves right), $x(2t)$ plays the signal twice as fast, like double-speed audio, and $x(-t)$ mirrors it. For $y(t)=x(at-b)$ the order matters: shift by $b$, then scale by $a$. Equivalently, $y(t)=x\!\left(a\left(t-\tfrac{b}{a}\right)\right)$ is $x$ compressed by $a$ and shifted by $b/a$, not by $b$.

$x(t)$

Shift: $x(t-2)$

Scale: $x(2t)$

Reversal: $x(-t)$

Unit step and ramp

$$u(t)=\begin{cases}1,&t\ge0\\0,&t<0\end{cases},\qquad r(t)=t\,u(t)$$

The example signal

$$x(t)=r(t)-r(t-1)-u(t-2)$$

The second ramp levels the signal off at 1; the step switches it off at $t=2$.

Even and odd parts

$$x_e(t)=\tfrac12[x(t)+x(-t)]$$$$x_o(t)=\tfrac12[x(t)-x(-t)]$$
Level M · SIG-M

Graphical Convolution: Flip and Slide

An LTI system answers each input value with a scaled copy of its impulse response and sums them. Graphically: reverse the impulse response, $h(-\tau)$, slide it to $t$, and the area under $x(\tau)h(t-\tau)$, shaded in the upper plot, is $y(t)$.

Convolution integral

$$y(t)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x(\tau)\,h(t-\tau)\,d\tau$$

Level C · SIG-C

Sampling, Aliasing and Reconstruction

An ADC samples $x(t)=\cos(2\pi f_0t)$ at $f_s$. If $f_s<2f_0$ (under two samples per cycle), the samples also fit a lower-frequency sinusoid, the alias, and nothing downstream can separate them. Theory predicts $f_a=|f_0-k f_s|$, where $k=\operatorname{round}(f_0/f_s)$: $f_0$ folds back from the nearest multiple of $f_s$. The DFT of the samples measures it.

Upper plot: true signal (light), samples (dots) and sinc reconstruction (rose). Reconstruction recovers $x(t)$ only when $f_s>2f_0$; otherwise it returns the alias. At the defaults the samples trace a 3 Hz alias; raise $f_s$ above 14 Hz. Since aliasing cannot be undone, ADCs use an anti-aliasing filter.

MATLAB reference script
f0 = 7; fs = 10; T = 4;
n = 0:round(T*fs)-1;  x = cos(2*pi*f0*n/fs);
w = hann(numel(x))';  Nfft = 2^16;
X = abs(fft(x.*w, Nfft));  f = (0:Nfft-1)*fs/Nfft;
[~,k] = max(X(1:Nfft/2));  fa_meas = f(k)
fa_pred = abs(f0 - round(f0/fs)*fs)
